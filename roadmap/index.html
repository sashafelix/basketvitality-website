Roadmap

A clearer picture. One release at a time.

Receipt scanning, in-store barcode checks and budgeting are already here. This roadmap shows what we would like to improve next, and what still needs exploration.

Android · Currently available in the Netherlands only.

Available today

Start with what is available today.

Free scanning

Scan before and after you shop

Scan a barcode in store to check available Nutri-Score, nutrition and Eco-Score. Scan a receipt afterwards to connect product information with your spending.

One category free

Budget with clear in-app warnings

Set a monthly category budget. See spent and remaining amounts, an orange indicator above 80% used and a red warning when you go over. Qualifying receipts connect budget and nutrition information.

Free + Pro

Put Smart Swaps to work

Review available alternatives, save a swap to your shopping list and see matched follow-through purchases. The existing basket report totals recorded savings and extra costs where prices are known.

Free includes unlimited receipt and barcode scanning, one budget category, the first two available Smart Swaps, seven days of receipt history and one lifetime bank-statement import. Pro adds full history, unlimited categories, the complete available swap list and unlimited statement imports.

Product data is incomplete. Eco-Score is shown where available on product screens; there is no complete environmental grade for every basket. Budget warnings are currently inside the app, not automatic phone notifications.

Planning

Proposed release sequence

These are planning candidates, not announced release dates. Scope, order and version numbers may change after testing and feedback. Only the section above describes features available now; tiers for future features are not yet set.

1.1 candidate

Next proposed release

Smoother everyday use

  • Bulk receipt import and editing, with progress and clear results for each receipt.
  • More relevant in-store alternatives using retailer context where it can be inferred reliably.
  • Receipt matching and category polish, plus a simpler monthly-to-yearly subscription change.

Focus: make the current Android experience easier to use before adding bigger workflows.

1.2 candidate

Following release

Digital receipts, less effort

  • Share a supported digital receipt into Basket Vitality from another Android app.
  • Read supported receipt formats on your phone, starting with Albert Heijn; add Jumbo when its formats are validated.
  • Handle unsupported documents clearly, without requiring access to your inbox.

Depends on representative receipt formats and reliable import results.

1.3 candidate

Following release

Understand what changes over time

  • Your own product price history, using comparable unit prices and confidently matched purchases.
  • Signals for pack-size changes and possible shrinkflation when there is enough reliable history.
  • Deeper spending and nutrition trends, an exportable weekly summary and optional budget notifications.

These extend the existing in-app warnings and swap report. Price history needs comparable purchases; notifications will need an explicit opt-in.

No release date

Exploring the longer term

These ideas have no release commitment. We will revisit them after learning from the Netherlands launch.

Connected banks & shared budgets

Explore bank connections and household budgeting. Both need a clear consent model, appropriate data handling and a sustainable cost model before a release is planned.

Germany & South Africa

Broader availability needs local product coverage, retailer receipt support and testing first. Translation or a store listing alone is not enough.

iOS — parked for now

Revisit an iPhone version after learning from the Netherlands Android launch.

Further ideas

Explore recovery options, cross-supermarket price comparisons and deeper on-device explanations. Feasibility and reliable data come before release promises.
