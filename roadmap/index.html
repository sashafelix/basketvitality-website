In the app today

Start with what's already there.

Free scanning

Scan before and after you shop

Scan a barcode at the shelf for Nutri-Score, Eco-Score and the nutrition per 100 g. Scan the receipt afterwards and every line is read on your phone, matched against more than 70,000 products and scored.

One category free

Budgets that read the receipt

A monthly budget per category: spent, remaining, orange past 80%, red when you're over. And the sentence that connects it to how the basket scored — "67% of your groceries budget, and 51% of it scored Nutri-D or worse."

Free + Pro

Smart Swaps, with the closed loop

A short list of the swaps worth making. Save one to your shopping list; the next receipt confirms whether you bought it and what it cost, both ways. The basket report nets savings against extra costs.

Free: unlimited receipt and barcode scanning, the top two swaps per basket, one budget category, seven days of history and one bank-statement import. Pro: your full history, unlimited categories, every swap and unlimited imports — €6,99 a month or €49,99 a year, with a 14-day trial.

1.1 — Smoother everyday use

Shipped · 16 September 2026

The sentence comes home

  • The sentence that connects budget and score, for the whole month, on the Home screen — not only on each receipt.
  • When a receipt line is matched loosely, the receipt says so — and one tap, "Yes, this is it", confirms the match and unlocks its swap.
  • Follow-through prices on Home and the shopping list both ways, so a swap that cost more is never hidden.
  • Plain-language labels in the fix-match list, and a rating prompt after your third scored basket — never on first launch.
  • Corrections are buttons — confirm a match, fix it, or re-file the line — and the app only offers a confirmation when it has checked that a swap would result.
  • The receipt says when its lines don't add up to the printed total, instead of presenting a partial basket as complete.

Shipped as 1.1.0 on 16 September 2026. Switching between monthly and yearly Pro follows on its own as 1.1.1 — today a subscriber has to cancel and re-subscribe to change plan.

1.1.1 — Plan switching

Right after 1.1

Change plan without cancelling

  • A "Change plan" row under Settings → Subscription. Monthly to yearly takes effect straight away with the unused days credited; yearly to monthly takes effect at the next renewal.

1.2 — Less typing, more receipts

Following release

Digital receipts, bulk import, better matching

  • Share a digital receipt into Basket Vitality from another Android app — Albert Heijn first, Jumbo once its formats are validated. Never by reading your inbox.
  • Import and edit receipts in bulk, with progress and a clear result per receipt.
  • Shelf suggestions that know which shop you're standing in.
  • A small on-device language model for the two places matching falls short today: loosely matched receipt lines, and products whose name hides the generic food behind it. It ships only if it measurably beats the current matcher on our real-receipt corpus.
  • More scores for more products. RIVM has confirmed our use of the NEVO food table in writing; we are requesting licences for further Dutch product databases so that more receipt lines get a grade.

Everything here stays on the phone. Digital receipts are read locally, the same as photographed ones.

1.3 — What changes over time

Following release

Your own price history

  • Price history per product from your own receipts, on comparable unit prices — and a signal when a pack shrinks while the price doesn't.
  • Spending and nutrition trends across months, and a weekly summary you can export.
  • Budget notifications on your phone, opt-in, on top of today's in-app warnings.

Price history needs enough of your own comparable purchases to be honest; shrinkflation signals need more still.

No release date

What we're still working out

No release is committed for these. We'll come back to them with what the Dutch launch teaches us.

Connected banks & shared budgets

Bank connections and household budgets. Both need a consent model we'd be proud of and a cost model that holds, before we plan a release.

Germany & South Africa

The product database is Dutch and doesn't transfer. A new country needs its own products and receipt formats first; translating the app is the easy part.

iOS — parked

The app is built on Kotlin Multiplatform, so an iPhone version is possible. We'll decide once the Android launch has taught us enough.

Further ideas

Recovery options for your encrypted data, price comparison across supermarkets, deeper on-device explanations. Reliable data comes before any of them.
